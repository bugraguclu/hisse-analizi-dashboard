## Motion

Use the tokens in `src/lib/motion.ts` for new motion and the shared motion preference for scripted movement. Prefer interruptible transform/opacity transitions. Press feedback starts on contact and activation occurs on release. Under reduced motion, apply spatial changes immediately and use opacity for feedback. Keep repeated data and list updates instant.
